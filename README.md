# Wikshi

Give your agent a voice, an inbox, and a way to reach the right people.

Wikshi helps agents research companies, find business contacts, send reviewed emails, make phone calls, and host guest video meetings of up to five minutes. Each paid operation has a quote, private results, and a signed receipt. Duration-based services reconcile actual usage and unused prepaid balance.

## Example mission

> Find four voice AI companies worth researching. Compare their products and customers, identify relevant business contacts, and draft a short introduction for my review.

Inbox creation and email sending are separate purchases. Research requests show progress and support cancellation. Phone calls and video meetings provide status, available transcripts, and usage-based receipts.

Payments use USDC or HBAR on Hedera testnet. Emails and calls reach real people, so review recipients and content before approving outreach. This is a testnet demonstration, not a production financial service.
